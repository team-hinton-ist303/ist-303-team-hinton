# Sprint 2 retrospective: team responses

Answers to the three retro questions, sent by message after the [Nov 11 stand-up](2026-11-11-standup.md). These feed section 12 (Retrospective) of the README.

1. What went well?
2. What didn't go well?
3. How can we improve?

## Yalei Xu (Aaron), Nov 11

1. **Went well:** The early rendering spike helped us choose the right tools, and teammates supported each other through PR reviews and testing.
2. **Didn't go well:** Some PR reviews took longer than expected, merge conflicts caused delays, and some Sprint 2 tests depended on other features being merged first.
3. **Improve:** Review PRs within 24 hours, make task dependencies clearer in Jira, and plan future work based on our actual velocity.
